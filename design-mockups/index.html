Architecture Note

Every surface color maps to a --vscode-* CSS variable first. Brand tint only appears as the 1px top-border accent on primary cards, the sparkline stroke, and the shield motif. The @vscode/webview-ui-toolkit was archived in January 2025 — these mockups depend on nothing but plain web standards and ship directly into the webview HTML.
